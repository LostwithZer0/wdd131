Trainquil Effects

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Trainquil Effects

Trainquil Effects is a house-call dog grooming and behavior consulting business. The unique spelling is intentional and creates a memorable brand while reflecting the business's focus on creating a calm and cooperative grooming experience for dogs.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Trainquil Effects website is to introduce potential clients to our house-call dog grooming services and explain how our approach differs from traditional salon or mobile-van grooming. The website will explain our cooperative care approach, provide information about grooming services, introduce our professional qualifications, answer common questions, share client experiences, and provide a way for potential clients to request an appointment.

Scenarios

  • How does house-call dog grooming work if Trainquil Effects does not use a grooming van?
  • What grooming services are available for my dog, and what is included?
  • How does Trainquil Effects help dogs that are nervous or uncomfortable during grooming?

Color Schema

Deep Blue Gray - #334E5C

Used for major headings, navigation, the footer, and other strong visual elements.

Muted Teal - #4F8F8B

Used as the primary brand color for buttons, links, interactive elements, and important accents.

Soft Aqua - #A8D5D1

Used for subtle backgrounds, cards, borders, and decorative accents.

Warm Ivory - #F7F5F0

Used as the primary page background to add warmth to the cooler blue and teal colors.

Charcoal - #29363B

Used for body text to provide strong readability and contrast.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond

Cormorant Garamond will be used for major headings and display text to give the website a warm and professional appearance.

Nunito Sans

Nunito Sans will be used for body text, navigation, buttons, forms, and other interface elements because it is clean and easy to read.
